**Recurring competitor features:**
- "Free", "no signup", "unlimited" — every competitor says this, but it's now a baseline expectation, not a differentiator
- "Check availability" — most claim to check, but several only do in-app TikTok checks, not API/live
- Multi-platform support (Instagram, YouTube, X) — common but mostly UI claim
- 6-15 categories, 5-8 styles, 12-30 ideas per gen — common cluster
- Display name vs username distinction — Postiz and FlowShorts lead with this

**Recurring competitor gaps (where we can win):**
- **Honest verdicts.** Every competitor either doesn't check or shows confident "Available / Taken" without a timestamp. Our "Likely available / Likely taken / Unknown" with timestamp is unique and defensible.
- **Per-vibe word banks, not random word lists.** Most generators pick random prefix + suffix. Our 17 vibes map to specific word pools.
- **17 languages with localized SEO.** No competitor (as of Aug 2026) ships a properly-localized TikTok name generator in more than 5 languages with hreflang and per-locale meta.
- **Per-locale keyword targeting.** We can rank for "tiktok username generator español" / "Generador de nombres TikTok" / "TikTok-Namensgenerator" etc.

## 5. Honest caveats

- **No per-market keyword tool.** I don't have Ahrefs / SEMrush / Google Keyword Planner access from this environment, so all the "Est. difficulty" / "Est. traffic" columns above are educated guesses based on SERP observation and competitor targeting. For a real launch, plug in real keyword data per market.
- **The "17 languages" localization is partial.** Tier 1 languages (8 of them) have full above-the-fold localization. Tier 2 languages (9 of them) have meta + nav localized but body in English. The SEO keywords in non-English languages are educated guesses — for a real launch in a specific market, have a native reviewer confirm the local keyword.
- **English keyword copy was applied.** All Tier 1 priority keywords (Clusters A, B, C, F, G) are now in the live English home, generator, FAQ, and About pages. You can verify by curl-ing the live URL and grepping for the keywords.
- **Competitor gap I see:** every competitor either doesn't actually check availability, or does a cheap in-app check that says "confident Available" without a timestamp. Our "Likely available" + timestamp is the most honest framing in the category and should be the cornerstone of the About page and the homepage hero. Don't bury it.
